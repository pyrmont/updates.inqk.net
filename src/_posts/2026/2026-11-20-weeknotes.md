- Jensen Huang, the Chief Executive Officer of Nvidia Corporation, resigned after media reports that he had had three affairs, one with a woman who may be connected to China's intelligence services ([ABC News](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-11-13/nvidia-founder-jensen-huang-resigns/107376470)). I have a longstanding grudge against Huang who I resent being held up as a visionary when he was hilariously wrong about the potential of the Metaverse (which he called the [Omniverse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nvidia_Omniverse)) and which everyone has forgotten about with the obvious exception of me. I had a long chat with sogaiu about where my anger at Huang comes from and the conclusion was that I don't like people getting credit when they don't deserve it. Honestly, though, that feels at least 50% a post-hoc rationalisation so I'm not sure. Maybe I just hate leather jackets.

- On Saturday morning I happened to see a promotional message on Japanese TV for a baseball game between Japan and Australia at Tokyo Dome later that day and found there were still inexpensive tickets for sale. I had a fun time when I went with the boys [back in September](https://updates.inqk.net/post/1788531060.html) so on the spur of the moment I decided to take them both again. We arrived just as the match was starting. Last time we sat right down near the dug out on the first base side of the diamond. This time I got seats out in the left outfield. I didn't appreciate that these are the seats where the fans shout and chant and while everyone was very polite it was a bit loud for the boys. We relocated to some much higher seats but not before the crowd around us went nuts watching Japan score 11 runs before Australia had managed to get a single player out. It was the worst I've ever seen any team be beaten at maybe anything in my life. Fortunately, the rest of the game wasn't quite as lopsided and we did see one Australian hit a home run.

- Later on Saturday night we let Rowan stay up to watch the Japan and Brazil friendly match that took place as part of the Kallang Football Series in Singapore. Japan got off to an early two-goal lead before Brazil clawed their way back in a way that looked like it might end with a heartbreaking Japanese loss (Brazil had similarly come back from a 1-0 deficit in the World Cup match against Japan). And then, just when it seemed like a draw was the best that Rowan could hope for, Japan scored a goal in additional time to win. The Brazilians were extremely upset at a referee who seemed completely out of his depth but it might have been the greatest moment so far in Rowan's life. 

- As grateful as I am to Singapore for giving my son so much joy, I cannot get over the ridiculousness of this event. It was ostensibly a celebration of the 60th anniversary of diplomatic relations between Japan and Singapore; a four-nation soccer tournament that featured Japan (naturally), Singapore (of course), Brazil (wait, what?) and Paraguay (_Paraguay_?). Japan played Paraguay and Brazil. Singapore played Paraguay and Brazil. Japan and Singapore did not play each other. I cannot stop thinking about the absurdity of all of this.
