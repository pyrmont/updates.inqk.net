- We went to DisneySea!

  - As discussed [previously](https://updates.inqk.net/post/1767967980.html), we (really, I think it’s me) like to go to Tokyo Disney Resort at least once a year. Last year, we realised in the final days of December that we hadn’t been but were able to squeeze in a last-minute visit to Disneyland on New Year’s Eve. I wanted to be a bit more organised this year and so I’ve been waiting for the right weekend for the past few weeks. Friday rolled around last week and the forecast for Sunday was pleasant, a car was available to hire and DisneySea was not sold out. I pounced and two days later we were there.

  - The day itself was fun. I commented to Eri that this might be one of the best visits we’ll ever have given that the kids are old enough to go on rides but not too old that they (a) don’t want to go on rides with their parents or (b) want to go on the more challenging rides that we (really, I think it’s me) don’t want to ride. As with my [previous visit](https://updates.inqk.net/post/1718116920.html), I think the stand-out ride for us was [Flouder’s Flying Fish Coaster](https://www.tokyodisneyresort.jp/en/tds/attraction/detail/237/), although an honourable mention goes to [Scuttle’s Scooters](https://www.tokyodisneyresort.jp/en/tds/attraction/detail/238/) which we road three times (!).
  
  - I was a little disappointed that [Tower of Terror](https://www.tokyodisneyresort.jp/en/tds/attraction/detail/243/) is out of service for all of October. I don’t think it’s a ride I’d enjoy but Eri and Emma might have liked to go on it and the fact it isn’t operational I think pushes up the wait times of some of the other popular rides (we didn’t even try to go on [Soaring](https://www.tokyodisneyresort.jp/en/tds/attraction/detail/219/) which had a consistent two-hour-plus wait throughout the entire day).

- I made another app! It’s in App Review so isn’t something publicly available but [Reportage](https://apps.inqk.net/reportage/) is meant to be a replacement for the venerable [Reporter](https://reporter-app.com). Like Reporter, Reportage is about answering surveys randomly throughout the day. I was amazed at how fast I was able to turn this one around. On Friday, I prototyped some web-based designs for the app and within a couple of hours, I had something working on my phone. This was my first time prototyping designs using Claude’s ‘artifacts’ system and it was a lot of fun. I did the same thing to work out the app icon. It’s difficult for me to imagine not working like this on future apps.
